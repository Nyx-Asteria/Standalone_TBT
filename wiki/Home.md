# Standalone TBT

Independent CarPlay and Android Auto navigation guidance for Hyundai/Kia Gen5W clusters and HUDs. English fork of Phone Projection TBT 1.0.5.

The upstream 1.0.5 baseline includes AA transient-distance filtering, corrected small-junction approaches, ordinary roundabout fallback when branches are absent, toll-state refresh, arrival-time formatting and removal of the CN7 PE start-handshake trial. The fork changes identity and language; hardware retesting is pending.
